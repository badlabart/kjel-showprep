National Pickle Day Pickles are sometimes served on a stick or even deep fried, and the day traces back to a 1949 International Pickle Week. That's a big dill.
SATURDAY 11/14
National Spicy Guacamole Day Guacamole means "avocado sauce" in Nahuatl, the Aztec language, and the Aztecs made the first batches between the 14th and 16th centuries. Dip into some history.

Clean Out Your Refrigerator Day Whirlpool created it in 1994 and put it in November so old ingredients get tossed before holiday cooking. Nothing ruins Thanksgiving like a science experiment.

National Bundt Day Nordic Ware, the maker of Bundt pans, started it in 2006 for its 60th anniversary, and some bakeries give out mini Bundt cakes. Free cake, no complaints.

Little Red Wagon Day Antonio Pasin started making little wagons in 1917 after customers kept asking to buy the one he hauled his tools in. Free advertising on wheels.

National Spicy Hermit Cookie Day Spicy hermit cookies may be named for how long they keep, like a hermit staying home, or for the brown robes hermits wore. Either way, homebodies.

Pack Your Mom's Lunch Day Lola's Diner created it in 2011 so kids could pack Mom's lunch for once. Hold the crusts.
SUNDAY 11/15
George Spelvin Day George Spelvin is the made-up name theater programs use when one actor plays two parts, so the same name doesn't show up twice. Fake name, real credit.
SUNDAY 11/15
America Recycles Day Only about 35% of waste in the United States is recycled, and this day started in 1997 to change that. Toss it in the right bin.
